# Milestone M2 — Encoders, Object Model, and Assemblers

Per [09-roadmap.md](../09-roadmap.md) (§M2) and [05-assembler.md](../05-assembler.md), Milestone M2 delivers the shared instruction encoding and disassembly tables (`Encode/`), the relocatable object model and ELF64 `ET_REL` reader/writer (`Object/`), and the multi-architecture assembler pipeline (`Asm/`) supporting x86-64 (AT&T syntax), AArch64, and RV64 (RVA23 profile).

---

## 1. Deliverables Completed

### 1.1 Table-Driven Instruction Encoders & Disassemblers (`Sources/SwiftStack/Encode/`)

All three target architectures use a single static instruction descriptor table shared between encoder and disassembler. Each descriptor is round-tripped (`encode -> disassemble -> re-encode`) from one sample, every sample is checked against `llvm-mc`, and for all three architectures every operand form of every descriptor is round-tripped and checked against `llvm-mc` (§2):

- **x86-64 (`X86Codec.swift`)**:
  - `X86InstructionTable`, `X86TableEncoder`, and `X86TableDisassembler`.
  - Covers baseline x86-64 GPR, system/privileged (`syscall`, `sysretq`, `swapgs`, `lgdt`, `lgdtl`, `lidt`, `ltr`, `str`, `invlpg`, `wrmsr`, `rdmsr`, `rdtsc`, `rdtscp`, `cpuid`, `wbinvd`, `fxsave64`, `fxrstor64`, `xsave64`, `xrstor64`, `xsaveopt64`, `xgetbv`, `xsetbv`, `rdpkru`, `wrpkru`, `clac`, `stac`, `monitor`, `mwait`, `%crN`/`%drN`/segment moves, port I/O), ABM/CRC32 (`lzcnt`, `tzcnt`, `popcnt`, `crc32b/w/l/q`), `endbr64`/`endbr32`, and the full `x86-64-v3` level of [17](../17-simd-and-vectorization.md) §2:
    - SSE through SSE4.2 (legacy encoding) and AVX/AVX2 (VEX.128 and VEX.256): moves (aligned/unaligned, scalar, `movd`/`movq`, half-register, non-temporal, `movmsk*`, `maskmov*`/`vpmaskmov*`), FP and integer arithmetic/logic, horizontal ops, compares (`cmpps $imm` plus all 32 `vcmp<pred>` pseudo-op spellings, `(u)comis*`, `pcmpeq*`/`pcmpgt*`, `pcmp[ei]str[im]`), shuffles/unpacks/packs/aligns, blends (immediate and variable), shifts (by `xmm`, by immediate, AVX2 per-element), conversions (`cvt*`, including the `x`/`y` memory spellings), rounding, dot products, insert/extract, broadcasts, `vperm*`/`vpermil*`, 128-bit lane insert/extract/permute, `ptest`/`vtestp*`, and AVX2 gathers (VSIB).
    - BMI1/BMI2 (`andn`, `bextr`, `blsi`, `blsmsk`, `blsr`, `bzhi`, `mulx`, `pdep`, `pext`, `rorx`, `sarx`, `shlx`, `shrx`), FMA3 (`vfmadd`/`vfmsub`/`vfnmadd`/`vfnmsub` 132/213/231 `ps`/`pd`/`ss`/`sd`, `vfmaddsub`/`vfmsubadd`), F16C, MOVBE, ADX, AES-NI and `pclmulqdq` (legacy and VEX.128), SHA, `movnti`, prefetch/`clflushopt`/`clwb`, `(v)ldmxcsr`/`(v)stmxcsr`.
    - VEX 2-byte vs 3-byte selection matches LLVM MC, including swapping the sources of commutable map-0F instructions to reach the 2-byte form (05 §4.11, difference 4).
    - **Not covered:** MMX forms, x87, AVX-512/EVEX, VAES/VPCLMULQDQ (256-bit), and AMD-only extensions (SSE4a, XOP, FMA4, TBM).
  - Supports `.code64`, `.code32`, and `.code16` real/protected-mode boot transition encodings (including `ljmpl`/`ljmpw` far jumps and 16-bit absolute addressing).
  - `X86InstructionTable.verifyRoundTrip()` round-trips every descriptor plus special addressing modes (`%rsp` SIB `0x24`, `%rbp` `mod=1 disp8=0`, scaled index without base, `%fs`/`%gs` segment overrides, `%rip`-relative, mandatory REX low-byte registers, high-byte registers, `lock`/`rep` prefixes, and 3-byte VEX extended registers).

- **AArch64 (`AArch64Codec.swift`)**:
  - `A64InstructionTable` (650 descriptors, up from 412 and originally 232), `A64Encoder`, and `A64Disassembler`. Descriptors whose width is a variable field (`size`, `ftype`, `Q`) list the scalar widths or vector arrangements they accept; the encoder rejects anything else, including out-of-range fields it used to mask (bitfield `immr`/`imms`, `extr`/`tbz` bit numbers, `hint`, `svc`/`brk` immediates, `nzcv`, PSTATE immediates). FP16 forms of the vector and scalar FP descriptors use the separate FP16 encoding class (`A64Encoder.fp16Pattern`), so one descriptor covers `.4h`/`.8h` (or `h`) and the single/double forms.
  - Integer: add/sub (immediate, shifted, extended), add/sub with carry (`adc`/`adcs`/`sbc`/`sbcs`, `ngc`/`ngcs`), logical (immediate, shifted), move wide, `adr`/`adrp`, bitfield, `extr`, 1/2/3-source data processing (with `cls`), `smulh`/`umulh`, widening multiplies (`smaddl`/`smsubl`/`umaddl`/`umsubl` and `smull`/`smnegl`/`umull`/`umnegl`), CRC32 (`crc32b`-`crc32cx`), conditional select and compare (`ccmp`, `ccmn`), branches.
  - Loads/stores: unsigned offset, pre/post-index, unscaled, unprivileged, register offset with every legal extend/shift, literal, `ldp`/`stp`, `ldnp`/`stnp`, exclusives (`ldxr`/`stxr`/`ldaxr`/`stlxr`/`ldar`/`stlr` with `b`/`h` forms, `ldxp`/`stxp`), RCpc `ldapr`/`ldaprb`/`ldaprh`, and the SIMD/FP (`b`/`h`/`s`/`d`/`q`) forms of these. Prefetch: `prfm` (unsigned offset, register offset, literal) and `prfum`, with every named operation or `#imm5`; the register-offset encodings with `Rt<4:3>` = 11 print as `rprfm`, as LLVM prints them.
  - LSE atomics: `ldadd`/`ldclr`/`ldeor`/`ldset`/`ldsmax`/`ldsmin`/`ldumax`/`ldumin`/`swp` and `cas` with `a`/`al`/`l` ordering and `b`/`h` sizes, `casp`, and the `st<op>` aliases.
  - Scalar FP (half, single, double): `fmov` (register, general, immediate), `fabs`, `fneg`, `fsqrt`, `frint[npmzaxi]`, `fcvt` between all precisions, `fcvt[npmza][su]`, `scvtf`/`ucvtf` (integer and fixed-point), `fcvtzs`/`fcvtzu` fixed-point, `fadd`/`fsub`/`fmul`/`fdiv`/`fnmul`, `fmax`/`fmin`/`fmaxnm`/`fminnm`, `fmadd`/`fmsub`/`fnmadd`/`fnmsub`, `fcmp`/`fcmpe` (register and `#0.0`), `fccmp`/`fccmpe`, `fcsel`.
  - Advanced SIMD, as a compositor and media codecs use it: three-same integer/bitwise/FP arithmetic and compares (halving and rounding adds, `saba`/`uaba`, saturating and rounding shifts by register, `smaxp`-`uminp`, `sqdmulh`/`sqrdmulh`, `sqrdmlah`/`sqrdmlsh`, `pmul`, `fmulx`, `frecps`/`frsqrts`, `facge`/`facgt`, the FP pairwise forms); three-different long, wide and narrow forms with their `2` variants (`[su]addl`, `[su]addw`, `[su]subl`/`w`, `[su]abal`, `[su]abdl`, `[su]mlal`, `[su]mlsl`, `[su]mull`, `sqdmlal`/`sqdmlsl`/`sqdmull`, `[r]addhn`, `[r]subhn`, `pmull`/`pmull2` including `1q`); by-element `mul`/`mla`/`mls`, `sq[r]dmulh`, `sqrdmlah`/`sqrdmlsh`, `fmul`/`fmla`/`fmls`/`fmulx` (vector and scalar), the long by-element forms and `sdot`/`udot`; dot product (vector and by element); permutes; two-register misc (including `sqabs`/`sqneg`, `suqadd`/`usqadd`, `urecpe`/`ursqrte`, `frecpe`/`frsqrte`, `fcvt[nmap][su]`), long pairwise `saddlp`/`uaddlp`/`sadalp`/`uadalp`, integer and FP compare with zero, `fcvtn`/`fcvtxn`/`fcvtl` (`2`); across lanes including `saddlv`/`uaddlv` and `fmaxv`/`fminv`/`fmaxnmv`/`fminnmv`; `xtn`, `sqxtn`/`uqxtn`/`sqxtun`; shifts by immediate (rounding, saturating, `sqshlu`, every narrowing `[r]shrn`/`sq[r]shr[u]n`/`uq[r]shrn`, `sshll`/`ushll` and the `sxtl`/`uxtl` aliases, fixed-point `scvtf`/`ucvtf`/`fcvtzs`/`fcvtzu`); scalar (`sisd`) FP conversions and estimates, `fabd`/`fmulx`/`frecps`/`frsqrts`/`fcm*`/`fac*`, compare with zero, and the `addp`/`faddp`/`fmaxp`/`fminp`/`fmaxnmp`/`fminnmp` reductions; `dup`/`ins`/`umov`/`smov` (with their `mov` aliases), `movi`/`mvni`/`orr`/`bic`/`fmov` (immediate, `fmov` also `.4h`/`.8h`), `ld1`-`ld4`/`st1`-`st4` (multiple structures, single lanes, with post-index), `ld1r`-`ld4r`, `tbl`/`tbx` (1-4 table registers), `ext`. FP16 (`.4h`/`.8h`, `h`) forms of every FP vector and scalar instruction above. Crypto: `aese`/`aesd`/`aesmc`/`aesimc`, `sha1c`/`sha1p`/`sha1m`/`sha1h`/`sha1su0`/`sha1su1`, `sha256h`/`sha256h2`/`sha256su0`/`sha256su1`.
  - System: `msr`/`mrs` (all system registers Todhchai and Croi named at the time, which VectraOS carries with croi's arm64 code, plus generic `s<op0>_<op1>_c<n>_c<m>_<op2>`), `msr` PSTATE (`spsel`, `daifset`, `daifclr`, `pan`), all 16 `dmb`/`dsb`/`isb`/`clrex` options (`ssbb`/`pssbb`), `hint` with its PAC/BTI/`csdb`/`dgh` aliases, `dc`/`ic`/`tlbi`/`at` (base-architecture and EL2 operations), exception generation, `udf`. SVE subset: `ldr`/`str` of `z`/`p` registers, `addvl`/`addpl`, `rdvl`, `rdffr`/`wrffr`.
  - The disassembler prints what `llvm-mc --disassemble` prints, including its preferred aliases (`mov`, `cmp`, `tst`, `neg`, `lsl`/`lsr`/`asr`, `ubfx`/`sbfiz`/`bfi`, `cset`/`cinc`, `mul`, `stadd`, `mvn`, `sxtl`, ...). The assembler accepts both these aliases and the architectural mnemonics.
  - Not in the table yet: `ldpsw`, generic `sys`/`sysl`, Armv8.3+ pointer-authentication branches and loads (`retaa`, `braa`, `ldraa`), `ldapur` (Armv8.4 RCpc2), FEAT_FHM (`fmlal`), scalar integer Advanced SIMD (`add d0, d1, d2`, `ushr d0, d1, #n`, ...) and the scalar saturating/doubling forms, the FP16 scalar pairwise forms (`faddp h0, v1.2h`), SM3/SM4/SHA512/SHA3, and SVE beyond VectraOS's subset. clang -O2 for `armv8.2-a+crc+dotprod+fp16+rcpc+aes+sha2` emitted none of these for the probe C (memcpy/memset loops, 128-bit arithmetic, atomics, bit manipulation, switch tables, compositor/codec loops, NEON/crypto intrinsics), all of which now assemble to the same `.text` as `clang -c`.

- **RV64 / RVA23 (`RV64Codec.swift`)**:
  - `RV64InstructionTable` (1,005 descriptors, up from 286), `RV64Encoder`, and `RV64Disassembler`. `RV64Encoder.validate` rejects what LLVM MC rejects: out-of-range or misaligned immediates, the RVC register restrictions, and the RVV 1.0 register-group alignment and overlap rules (destination vs. `v0` mask, widening/narrowing and gather/slide-up/compress sources).
  - Covers every instruction-bearing extension of the ratified RVA23U64 and RVA23S64 profiles:
    - RV64I, M, A, F, D, C, Zicsr, Zifencei, B (Zba, Zbb, Zbs), Zicond, Zicbom, Zicboz, Zicbop (`prefetch.i/r/w`), Zihintpause (`pause`), Zihintntl (`ntl.*`, `c.ntl.*`), Zawrs (`wrs.nto`, `wrs.sto`), Zimop/Zcmop (`mop.r.N`, `mop.rr.N`, `c.mop.N`), Zfa, Zfhmin (`flh`/`fsh`, `fmv.x.h`/`fmv.h.x`, the `h` converts), and Zcb.
    - V (RVV 1.0) in full: `vset{i}vl{i}`; unit-stride, strided, indexed (ordered and unordered), segment (`nf` 2-8), whole-register, mask and fault-only-first loads and stores; integer, fixed-point, floating-point, widening and narrowing arithmetic; multiply-add; compares; reductions; mask operations; slides, gathers, `vcompress`, merges and scalar moves; conversions. Zvbb/Zvkb (`vandn`, `vbrev`, `vbrev8`, `vrev8`, `vclz`, `vctz`, `vcpop.v`, `vrol`, `vror`, `vwsll`) and Zvfhmin. Zkt and Zvkt add no instructions.
    - Supervisor and hypervisor: `sret`, `wfi`, `sfence.vma`, Svinval (`sinval.vma`, `sfence.w.inval`, `sfence.inval.ir`, `hinval.vvma`, `hinval.gvma`), and H (`hfence.vvma`, `hfence.gvma`, `hlv`/`hlvx`/`hsv` in all widths); `mret` for M-mode code.
    - Every CSR name LLVM MC prints for RV64, including Sstc (`stimecmp`, `vstimecmp`), Sscofpmf (`scountovf`), Ssstateen, Smaia/Ssaia and the H/VS-mode CSRs. Other CSR numbers print in decimal, as LLVM MC prints them.
  - Every encoding LLVM MC decodes with `-mattr=+rva23s64` decodes to the same instruction, checked over all 16-bit words and a sample of about 2.9 million 32-bit words (checked once while developing the tables; not a permanent test). The only exception is `dret` (Sdext debug mode), which is not part of RVA23.
  - The disassembler prints what `llvm-objdump` prints: a compressed instruction as its 32-bit expansion, then LLVM MC's preferred alias (`li`, `mv`, `nop`, `ret`, `j`, `jr`, `beqz`, `seqz`, `sext.w`, `zext.b`, `csrr`, `csrw`, `rdcycle`, `frflags`, `fsrmi`, `fmv.d`, `fneg.s`, `vl1r.v`, `vneg.v`, `vnot.v`, `vfneg.v`, `vwcvt.x.x.v`, `vncvt.x.x.w`, `vmmv.m`, `vmclr.m`, ...). HINT encodings (`c.nop 5`, `c.li zero, 3`, `c.slli a0, 0`, ...) print compressed. `RV64Disassembler.format(_:aliases: false)` gives the canonical form under the instruction's own mnemonic. The assembler accepts both forms, and the explicit `c.*` mnemonics.
  - `RV64InstructionTable.verifyRoundTrip()` round-trips every descriptor template (both 32-bit and 16-bit compressed encodings), and `M2RV64ExhaustiveTests.swift` round-trips every operand form (§2).
  - **Not covered:** optional and non-RVA23 extensions (Zfh/Zvfh arithmetic, Zfbfmin/Zvfbf*, scalar and vector crypto other than Zvkb, Zbc, Zacas, Zabha, Zicfilp/Zicfiss, Zcmp/Zcmt, Sdext's `dret`) and RV32-only forms. The assembler compresses with C only. With `.option rvc` it does not apply the Zcb compressions (`c.mul`, `c.zext.b`, `c.not`, `c.lbu`, ...) that LLVM MC applies with `+zcb`, because `RVAsmOptions` has no Zcb switch yet. VectraOS's `rv64imac` target does not have Zcb. `RV64Encoder.encode(_:rvcEnabled: true)` does apply them, as LLVM MC does under RVA23.

### 1.2 Relocatable Object Model & ELF64 Reader/Writer (`Sources/SwiftStack/Object/`)

- **`RelocatableObject.swift`**:
  - `~Copyable` Structure-of-Arrays (`SectionColumn`, `SymbolColumn`, `RelocationColumn`) produced by the assembler (`Asm`). The M0.5 code generator now encodes every instruction through `X86TableEncoder` (`Codegen/X86CodeEmitter.swift`; the hand-rolled `Encode/X86Encoder` is gone) but still links through `Linker/StaticELFWriter`; moving it onto `RelocatableObject` is M3 work.
  - Closed `ObjectArch` (`x86_64`, `aarch64`, `riscv64`) and `ObjectFormat` (`elf64`) enums.
- **`ELFObjectIO.swift`**:
  - `ELFObjectWriter`: Deterministic (`D6`) ELF64 `ET_REL` serializer with canonical section ordering, local-before-global `SHT_SYMTAB` partitioning (`sh_info` = first global index), per-code-section `.rela<sec>` tables, and architecture `e_flags` (`EF_RISCV_RVC | EF_RISCV_FLOAT_ABI_DOUBLE` on RV64).
  - `ELFObjectReader`: Full ELF64 `ET_REL` parser reconstructing `RelocatableObject` sections, symbols, and `.rela` relocations for round-trip verification and upcoming M3 linker input. Input is untrusted: every offset, size, index, `sh_link`/`sh_info` and string reference is validated with overflow-checked arithmetic, and malformed input throws an `ELFReadError` instead of trapping.

### 1.3 Multi-Architecture Assemblers & `.S` Preprocessing (`Sources/SwiftStack/Asm/`)

- **`CPreprocessor.swift` (`assemblerMode: true`)**:
  - Preserves newlines after `#include`, `#define`, and `#if`/`#ifdef` directives so line-oriented assembly statements and line numbers remain intact.
  - Preserves `#` immediate prefixes on AArch64 (`#0x10`) inside macro bodies rather than treating them as C stringification operators when not followed by a macro parameter name.
- **`Assembler.swift`**:
  - Shared directive engine (`.section`, `.text`, `.data`, `.rodata`, `.bss`, `.pushsection`, `.popsection`, `.globl`/`.global`, `.weak`, `.local`, `.hidden`, `.type`, `.size`, `.equ`/`.set`, `.byte`, `.short`/`.hword`/`.2byte`, `.word`/`.long`/`.4byte`, `.quad`/`.dword`/`.8byte`, `.asciz`/`.string`, `.ascii`, `.space`/`.skip`/`.zero`, `.align`/`.balign`/`.p2align`, `.cfi_*`, `.option`, `.arch`, `.code16`/`.code32`/`.code64`).
  - Macro & repetition preprocessor (`.macro`/`.endm` with `\@` unique invocation counters, default/`:req`/`:vararg` parameters and `\()`, `.rept`/`.endr`, `.irp`/`.endr`, `.if`/`.ifdef`/`.ifndef`/`.ifeq`/`.ifne`/`.else`/`.elseif`/`.endif`) and directional numeric local labels (`1:` with `1b` / `1f` resolution).
  - Multi-pass branch relaxation engine with architecture-specific `NOP` alignment padding (multi-byte `NOP`s on x86-64, `0xD503201F` on AArch64, `0x00000013` / `0x0001` on RV64).
- **Architecture Frontends**:
  - `X86Assembler.swift` (`X86Asm`): AT&T syntax, `%seg:disp(base, index, scale)` parsing, `@PLT` / `@GOTPCREL` / `@TPOFF` modifiers, `.code16`/`.code32`/`.code64` state switching, and x86-64 `JMP`/`Jcc` `rel8` <-> `rel32` relaxation.
  - `AArch64Assembler.swift` (`A64Asm`): A64 register/NEON/SVE syntax, `:pg_hi21:` / `:lo12:` / `:got:` / `:got_lo12:` relocation modifiers, `adr`/`adrp`, `mov` wide/inverted/bitmask immediate synthesis, and `R_AARCH64_*` fixup emission.
  - `RV64Assembler.swift` (`RVAsm`): RV64I/M/A/F/D/C/V + RVA23 syntax, `.option rvc`/`norvc`/`push`/`pop`, `%pcrel_hi`/`%pcrel_lo`/`%hi`/`%lo` modifiers, pseudo-instructions (`la`, `lla`, `li` full 64-bit synthesis, `call`, `tail`, `j`, `jr`, `ret`, `mv`, `not`, `neg`, `negw`, `sext.w`, `seqz`, `snez`, `sltz`, `sgtz`, `beqz`, `bnez`, `blez`, `bgez`, `bltz`, `bgtz`, `bgt`, `ble`, `bgtu`, `bleu`, `csrr`, `csrw`, `csrs`, `csrc`, `csrwi`, `csrsi`, `csrci`, `rdcycle`, `rdtime`, `rdinstret`, `frflags`/`fsflags`/`fsflagsi`, `frrm`/`fsrm`/`fsrmi`, `fmv`/`fneg`/`fabs`, and every RVV alias the disassembler prints), explicit `c.*` mnemonics, and RVC opportunism (`EB8`).

## 2. Exit Criteria Verification

Per [09-roadmap.md](../09-roadmap.md) (§M2) and [05-assembler.md](../05-assembler.md) §4.11:

1. **Assembly matches LLVM MC, the reference assembler; differences from GNU `as` are documented.** `M2DifferentialTests.swift`:
   - Each file is assembled by Swift-Stack and by the oracles. Both objects are parsed by an independent test-only ELF reader, and the comparison covers every allocatable section (bytes, size, flags, alignment), every relocation, and every non-temporary symbol.
   - **Corpora:** the Todhchai floor entry stubs (3), all 21 Croi `kernel/arch/{amd64,arm64,rv64}/*.S` files, and the in-repo regression corpus `Tests/Differential/asm/<arch>/`. *(As measured at the time. Todhchai and croi have since been retired, and the corpus is now VectraOS's 26 `.S` files; see "Corpus moved to VectraOS" below.)*
   - **Oracles:** `clang -c` for all three architectures, compared exactly (LLVM MC is the reference, 05 §4.11), and GNU `as` (via `gcc -c`) for x86-64. No GNU cross-assembler for AArch64/RV64 is installed on the reference machine.
   - **GNU `as` differences:** the corpus differences are the ones documented in 05 §4.11: alignment, NOP padding and `disp32` for a late-resolved label difference (x86-64 encoding); `.eh_frame` FDE order and CIE sharing (`cfi_cie_order.s`, whose `.eh_frame` is exempted); and macro argument splitting (`macro_args.s`, which GNU `as` rejects, so it is compared with clang only). Each is exempted only for the affected section or file, and the clang comparison still covers it exactly.
   - A missing corpus or oracle skips the test rather than passing it.
2. **Encoder/disassembler round trip.** `verifyRoundTrip()` round-trips one sample per descriptor. For x86-64 the round trip is exhaustive over the instruction table: `M2X86ExhaustiveTests.swift` (05 §4.11) generates every operand form of each of the 1,583 descriptors (about 285,000 instructions: every register of each slot's class, all low/extended register combinations, every ModR/M/SIB special case, boundary immediates). Each must round-trip, match `llvm-mc` byte for byte from both its text and our disassembly, assemble identically through `Asm/`, and match GNU `as` apart from the documented differences. The test also asserts that every descriptor is selected at least once. For AArch64, `M2AArch64ExhaustiveTests.swift` does the same for all 650 descriptors (about 261,000 instructions: every width and arrangement a descriptor accepts, every register slot swept over all 32 encodings, every addressing mode and extend, all conditions, every lane of the by-element and single-structure forms, all bitmask immediates, all `immr:imms` pairs, all FP8 immediates), against `llvm-mc -mattr=+lse,+rdm,+rcpc,+crc,+fullfp16,+dotprod,+aes,+sha2,...`. Each must round-trip to the same descriptor, assemble through `llvm-mc` from our disassembly to exactly our bytes, disassemble through `llvm-mc` to exactly our text, and assemble identically through `Asm/`. Forms LLVM rejects as unpredictable (writeback base equal to a transfer register, `ldp` with Rt == Rt2, an exclusive store whose status register is also a source) are not generated. A rejection pass mutates up to four forms of each descriptor into about 31,600 representative invalid ones (wrong arrangement or element width, out-of-range immediate or lane, wrong register width or class, a W base, an extra operand); `llvm-mc` and our assembler must agree on every one, and each descriptor must have mutations both reject (05 §4.11 lists the invalid forms llvm-mc 22 accepts and alters, which it excuses). Each pass sends all its lines to one `llvm-mc` invocation; in a debug build the round-trip pass takes about 17 s (10 s before this extension) and the rejection pass about 4 s, run in parallel. For RV64, `M2RV64ExhaustiveTests.swift` does the same for all 1,005 descriptors (about 350,000 instances, 345,000 of them valid). Each register slot is swept over its whole file (x0-x31, f0-f31, v0-v31, or x8-x15 for the 3-bit RVC fields), masked and unmasked. Other inputs: every rounding mode and `aq`/`rl` pair; every value of immediates up to 8 bits, and the minimum, maximum, single-bit and alternating-bit values (with alignment) of wider ones; all 2,048 `vsetvli` and 1,024 `vsetivli` `vtypei` values, so every SEW, LMUL, ta/tu and ma/mu combination plus the reserved encodings; every CSR number, `fence` set pair and `fli` constant; the RVC HINT forms; register ties and x0 operands for the aliases; and register/immediate combinations at every RVC boundary. Each instance must round-trip to identical bytes, with and without RVC. `llvm-mc -mattr=+rva23s64` must assemble its canonical and alias text to exactly our encoding with RVC enabled, and the same with C/Zca/Zcb/Zcd/Zcmop disabled to our uncompressed encoding: compressed vs. uncompressed must agree in both cases. `llvm-mc --disassemble` must print exactly our text. Forms our encoder rejects (RVV register-group and overlap rules) must be rejected by `llvm-mc` too, and Swift-Stack's assembler must reproduce every valid form and reject every invalid one. That is 1.37 million `llvm-mc` encodings, 345,000 disassemblies and 689,000 assembler lines. Every descriptor must round-trip at least once. `M2EncoderOracleTests.swift` checks every sample against `llvm-mc`, which catches encoder/disassembler pairs that agree on a wrong encoding (it found the 16-bit `tzcnt`/`lzcnt`/`popcnt` prefix bug).
3. **Behavior without an oracle.** `M2AssemblerRegressionTests.swift` covers out-of-range AArch64/RV64 branch relaxation (clang reports these as errors instead of relaxing), rejected encodings being diagnosed rather than dropped, `ldr Xt, #imm`, rendered diagnostic locations (`file:line:col` through `.include`, macros, `.rept` and `.S` preprocessing), and an ELF reader fuzz test (every truncation plus 12,000 seeded mutations of valid objects must throw, never crash). It also pins LLVM MC output for CFI (including FDE grouping by CIE), `.equ` label differences, expression precedence and macro and `.irp` arguments, and LLVM MC's macro and CFI diagnostics, so these hold where no oracle is installed.

### Defects fixed after the initial M2 report
- **`.long sym - .`:** produced an absolute relocation. It now uses `R_X86_64_PC32`/`PC64`, `R_AARCH64_PREL32`/`PREL64`, or an `R_RISCV_ADD`/`SUB` pair.
- **`.size sym, . - sym`:** produced 0.
- **`.set` symbols:** were not emitted as `SHN_ABS`.
- **AArch64/RV64 branches out of range:** were silently truncated. They are now relaxed to an inverted branch over `b`/`jal`, as §4.5 specifies, and diagnosed if out of range even then.
- **AArch64 encoder rejections:** an instruction the encoder rejected was silently dropped. The encoder now range-checks PC-relative fields.
- **AArch64 operand checking** (found by the rejection pass of the exhaustive sweep): the hand-written parsers accepted what LLVM MC rejects and encoded something else, or trapped. A W register in an X slot or base (`add x0, w1, #0`, `ldr x0, [w1]`, `stxr x3, w1, [x2]`), mixed pair widths (`ldp x0, w1, ...`; `ldp x5, s6, ...` even assembled as `ldrsw`), a register spelled as an immediate (`add w1, w2, s3` became a `:lo12:` relocation against a symbol `s3`), out-of-range `tbz` bits, `movk`/`hint`/`svc` immediates and bitfield widths (masked, or an arithmetic trap in `ubfx`/`bfi`), an unaligned `adrp` displacement, `#-0.0` in FP compares with zero, `mov w0, v1.b[0]`, `movi v0.1d`, pre/post-indexed `ldur`/`ldtr`, operands on `nop`/`eret`/`ssbb`, and a W or missing register on `tlbi`/`dc`/`at`/`mrs`/`msr`/`br`/`ret`. Each is now an `assembler.invalid_operands` error, as is any parsed form the encoder rejects. `msr spsel, #imm` takes 0-15, as in LLVM.
- **`.note.gnu.property` section type:** a `.pushsection .note.gnu.property, "a"` (how hand-written BTI/PAC `.S` files mark themselves) produced `SHT_PROGBITS`, which linkers ignore, so a program linked from Swift-Stack objects lost its GNU_PROPERTY_AARCH64_FEATURE_1_AND marking. A `.note*` section without an explicit type is now `SHT_NOTE`, as in LLVM MC; `Tests/Differential/asm/aarch64/bti_note.S` checks it against `clang -c`, and the differential comparison now checks `sh_type` (processor-specific types aside).
- **`.S` target macros:** AArch64/RV64 `.S` files also saw `__x86_64__`.
- **RISC-V external `j`:** a compressed jump to an external symbol was laid out at 2 bytes but emitted at 4.
- **`.eh_frame`:** CFA instructions had no `DW_CFA_advance_loc*`, so every rule applied from the function start. Records now match LLVM MC byte for byte (advance_loc/1/2/4, FDEs grouped by CIE key with one CIE per group, padding), and more `.cfi_*` directives are supported (05 §4.4, §4.9).
- **`.equ`/`.set` of a label difference:** did not resolve to a constant when it named labels, especially forward ones. It now resolves after layout, in immediates, data and `.size`.
- **Expression precedence:** followed C. It now follows LLVM MC's ELF precedence, which GNU `as` shares (05 §4.4), including -1 for true comparisons and binary `!`.
- **Macros:** `:vararg` parameters, `\()` concatenation and argument splitting were missing or wrong; unbounded recursion overflowed the stack.
- **GNU-preferring choices:** `.eh_frame` kept FDEs in source order and macro arguments were split as GNU `as` splits them. Both now match LLVM MC (05 §4.11, differences 8–11), as do `.irp`/`.irpc` value lists, quoted macro arguments (no escape processing), the 20-level nesting limit, and LLVM MC's macro and CFI diagnostics, which were previously accepted silently. `\@` counted from 1 instead of 0. A `.set` of a fixed label difference in another section was not folded at once, so a later reassignment changed earlier uses.
- **Diagnostic locations:** assembler errors reported a bare line number, with no file, and the wrong line after `.include`, macro expansion and `.S` preprocessing. They now carry `file:line:col`, with notes at macro uses.
- **ELF reader:** truncated or corrupted objects (bad offsets, sizes, `sh_link`/`sh_info`, string offsets, symbol/relocation indices) could trap.
- **RVC:** `addi sp, sp, -16` used `c.addi16sp`; GNU `as` and LLVM MC use `c.addi` when the immediate fits.
- **Diagnostic rendering** trapped on an empty `DiagnosticBag`.

### Corpus moved to VectraOS

The Todhchai and croi corpora above were retired with those projects. VectraOS, which carries croi's kernel and is now the first-party consumer ([00](../00-overview-and-principles.md) §7), replaced them. `M2DifferentialTests.swift` assembles all 26 of its `.S` files for their architectures with the definitions, include paths and ISA flags of its CMake build (`SWIFTSTACK_VECTRAOS_ROOT`, default `~/Projects/OS/VectraOS-swift`): 30 objects, 10 per architecture, all matching `clang -c` exactly, and the 10 x86-64 objects also matching GNU `as` with the documented allowances. VectraOS builds its RV64 kernel for `rv64imac_zicsr_zifencei`, PIE, with clang's default linker relaxation, which the croi corpus had been assembled without. Defects this exposed, each now fixed with a regression test:

- **`A - B` with `B` in the current section and `A` undefined** (`.long <entry point> - vdso_header` in the vDSO header) was an error on x86-64 and AArch64. It is now a PC-relative relocation against `A` with `P - B` in the addend, as in LLVM MC.
- **RISC-V linker relaxation** was not implemented (`.option relax` behaved as `norelax`). The assembler now emits `R_RISCV_RELAX` after relaxable relocations, worst-case NOP padding with `R_RISCV_ALIGN` for code alignment, and keeps branches and label differences that span either as relocations, matching LLVM MC ([05](../05-assembler.md) §4.8).
- **RISC-V target options:** the assembler assumed RVA23 and non-PIC. `RVAsmOptions(march:pic:relax:)` now takes them from the target as clang's driver does; without Zbs, `li` of a single high bit no longer uses `bseti`, and with `-fPIE`, `la` goes through the GOT.
- **RISC-V stores with `%lo`/`%tprel_lo`** used the I-type relocation (`R_RISCV_LO12_I`) instead of the S-type one.
- **RISC-V NOP padding** put the `c.nop` after the 4-byte `nop`s; LLVM MC puts it first.

### clang's own assembly output (desktop path)

VectraOS's V6 desktop targets x86-64 PCs and an Armv8.2-A board, with arm64 user code built with `-mbranch-protection=bti`. `M2ClangRoundTripTests.swift` compiles every VectraOS C file with `clang-22 -S` for each architecture with the flags of its CMake build, at -O0 and -O2 (104 objects: arm64 with `-mbranch-protection=bti`, and the kernel's `trace.c` also with its `pac-ret+bti`), assembles the `.s` with Swift-Stack and with `clang -c`, and compares the objects exactly (`ExactObjectComparison`: every section including non-allocatable and empty ones with type, flags, alignment and entry size, every relocation with the symbol it names, every symbol including `.L*`, mapping, file and section symbols, and `e_flags`). The in-repo corpus and the 30 VectraOS `.S` objects are now compared the same way as well. The tests use `clang-22` when it is installed, because a toolchain's older `clang` may come first on `PATH`; LLVM MC 22 is the reference. Gaps this closed:

- **Directives clang emits:** AArch64 `.aeabi_subsection`/`.aeabi_attribute` (the `.ARM.attributes` build attributes, `SHT_AARCH64_ATTRIBUTES`, with LLVM MC's checks and diagnostics) and `.xword`; RISC-V `.attribute` (`.riscv.attributes`; `arch` also resets the extensions in effect) and `.half`; `.file` (`STT_FILE`), `.ident` (`.comment`), `.addrsig`/`.addrsig_sym` (`.llvm_addrsig`); `sh_entsize` of mergeable sections. `.dword` is now rejected on x86-64, as in LLVM MC.
- **Symbols:** AArch64 and RISC-V mapping symbols (`$x`/`$d`, including LLVM 22's RISC-V state after `.popsection`); `STT_TLS` for symbols in TLS sections; an alias (`busy = .L_MergedGlobals+4`) takes its target's type.
- **Relocation targets** ([05](../05-assembler.md) §4.5.1): relocations against local symbols now use the section symbol plus offset on x86-64 and AArch64 (the `.L` symbol is then not in `.symtab`), with LLVM MC's exceptions; RISC-V keeps every symbol, with LLVM's names for temporaries. A branch to a global symbol in its own section is relocated instead of resolved, on all three targets.
- **RISC-V relaxation (LLVM MC 22):** forward branches to local labels keep relocations; a backward one across another branch is relocated; `R_RISCV_JAL` and `%got_pcrel_hi` get `R_RISCV_RELAX`; code alignment is relaxed only after a branch or relaxable instruction in its section; a branch to another section, a global or an undefined symbol is relaxed to an inverted branch (compressed when possible) over `jal`. `fld`/`fsd`-family loads and stores take `%lo`/`%pcrel_lo` operands. `e_flags` and the driver's `Tag_RISCV_arch` follow `-march`/`-mabi` (`RVAsmOptions(march:pic:relax:abi:)`).
- **x86-64:** NOP padding uses LLVM MC's 10-byte `nopw %cs:` form; GOT loads LLVM MC can relax are `R_X86_64_(REX_)GOTPCRELX`; `.eh_frame` is `SHT_X86_64_UNWIND`.
- **AArch64:** `add ..., :tprel_hi12:sym` selects the `lsl #12` form.
- **`.rept`** takes an absolute expression that is a difference of labels already laid out in one run of fixed-size fragments (`.rept (end - start) / 4`); otherwise LLVM MC's `unexpected token in '.rept' directive` (and `Count is negative`). Statements are now assembled as the macro engine produces them, so the count sees the layout so far.
- **`.include` recursion** stopped at 64 levels with a misleading `cannot open`; it is now `'.include' nested too deeply (more than 64 levels)`, with an `in file included from here` note per level (LLVM MC itself recurses without bound).

Not done: the shared `Encode` tables lack x86-64 `shld`/`shrd` (`int128.c`, `vdso.c`) and `xadd` (`usertest.c` at -O0), and the SVE `index`/`cntd`/`ptrue`/`lastb` of `usertest.c`'s inline assembly; these 9 of the 104 round-trip objects are recorded as known issues. Swift-Stack does not normalize a hand-written `.attribute arch` string (clang's output is already normalized), names `.`-temporaries for RISC-V `.L0 ` without LLVM's counter details, and does not synthesize DWARF from `.file`/`.loc` (`-g`).
