## 5. Table Layout Confirmations (18-data-catalog.md)

1. **`TokenBuffer`**: 10 bytes/row SoA (`kinds`, `flags`, `starts`, `ends`). Confirmed.
2. **`ParseTree`**: 9 bytes/row SoA (`kinds: UInt8`, `tokens: UInt32`, `subtreeSizes: UInt32`). Confirmed.
3. **`AIRFunction`**: 17 bytes/row SoA (`opcode`, `type`, `op0`, `op1`, `aux`) confirmed for middle-end pass filtering; 20 bytes/row AoS evaluated.
4. **`LIRFunction`**: 28 bytes/row SoA (`opcodes`, `dst`, `src1`, `src2`, `imm`, `flags`, `extra`) confirmed for M0.5 slice; 32 bytes/row AoS confirmed as revision target for M2/M3 table-driven encoder.
5. **D6 Determinism**: 100% byte-for-byte identical output verified across runs via `fnv1a16` symbol hashing and source-order declaration tracking.
